Herbal Shampoo

A plant-based shampoo that supports growth by clearing blockages and reducing shedding, for thicker hair and retained length. Organic DHT inhibitors such as pumpkin seed, saw palmetto and green tea extract do the work, while aloe vera and essential oils calm an itchy scalp and keep hair soft and manageable.

  • Natural DHT blockers
  • No sulfates, parabens or DEA
  • 8 fl oz / 240 ml

How to use+

Thoroughly wet hair and scalp. Work a quarter-sized amount between your hands, then apply it to the scalp with your fingers, parting the hair so it reaches every area. Let it sit on the scalp for a few minutes, then rinse thoroughly. Repeat if desired, and follow with Alchemy Herbal Conditioner.

Key ingredients+

Pumpkin seed oil, saw palmetto extract and organic green tea extract, natural DHT blockers that help clear what can slow growth. Aloe vera, rosemary, peppermint and tea tree soothe and nourish the scalp, with biotin and niacinamide.
