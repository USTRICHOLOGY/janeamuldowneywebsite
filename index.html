Questions

Good to know before you book.

Have a question that is not here? Call (760) 716-0335 or send a message on Instagram.

What happens at my first consultation?+

We talk through your history, habits and what you have been noticing. Then JaNea looks at your scalp up close with a magnified scope to see what is happening at the follicle. You leave with a clear plan for next steps.

How is this different from a head spa?+

A head spa is a lovely cleanse and a chance to relax. As a certified trichologist, JaNea focuses on understanding what is causing your hair or scalp concern and treating it at the source. You still get a calm, relaxing experience.

Do the treatments use needles?+

No. Treatments are non-invasive, including how growth factors and exosomes are delivered to the scalp.

How many sessions will I need?+

It depends on your concern and how your scalp responds. After your consultation, JaNea will recommend a plan and adjust it at each follow-up as you progress.

Can men book too?+

Yes. Scalp and hair concerns are not only for women, and every treatment is tailored to the person in the chair.

I am shedding while on a GLP-1. Can you help?+

Shedding during rapid weight loss is something JaNea sees often. A consultation helps sort out what is behind it and how to best support your scalp and hair through it.
